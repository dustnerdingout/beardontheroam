Chapter 01

Back then

Growing up, the outdoors was the whole world.

Long days outside, muddy boots, and a fishing pole that went everywhere with me. If there was a creek, a back road, or a trail nobody else was taking, that's where I wanted to be. Adventure didn't need a plan back then. It just needed daylight.

Fishing was a big part of it. Early mornings at the water, waiting on a bite, learning patience without realizing that's what I was learning. Those were some of the best days I've had.

Chapter 02

Getting older

Then life happened like it does for everybody. Work, responsibilities, a calendar that fills itself. The trips got shorter. The rod sat in the corner longer than it should have.

I didn't want those days to just be memories.

As I got older, I realized I wanted to relive it. Get back on the trails, get back on the water, and feel that same pull to see what's around the next bend. Not someday. Now.

Chapter 03

Why share it

The best parts of being outside were always better with someone else there. So Beard on the Roam is about bringing people along: the trail runs, the fishing trips, the camp nights, and every wrong turn that turned into a good story.

Maybe you grew up the same way and miss it. Maybe you've never been off the pavement and want to start. Either way, this is for you. Come along, pick up a few tips, and go make your own trips happen.

Chapter 04

Get lost

That's the motto. That's the project.

Pick a road you haven't taken. Follow it until the signal drops. Find water, wet a line, and see what the day turns into. Then come back and tell somebody about it.
